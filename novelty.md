# Novelty gate

2026-10-29: **insufficient-evidence** for a new algorithm; continue research within A2.

Rejected novelty: executing alternatives before clarification (AIR), finite-world answer-class selection (EC2), missing-interpretation infilling (Disambiguate First, Parse Later), discovering identical AmbiQT columns (explicitly documented in that work).

Provisional question: When data repairs can change answer equivalence, what representation and decision rule preserve necessary interpretations without enumerating all repaired databases? Can execution witnesses diagnose premature stopping under realistic candidate omission and uncertain feedback?

Required falsifiers: a simple robust EC2 baseline with the same candidate-generation budget; exact small-world optimum; native task capability; controlled and naturally occurring anomalies separately; cost and coverage audits; independent confirmation data. Demonstrating that a deliberately irreversible pruning ablation fails under a constructed swap is not sufficient.

Follow-up E007–E009: structured score inference is a known strong baseline, and
strict context-matched relevance filtering must also be supplied to controls.
The exact four-cell binary comparison bound improves execution count over the
older relaxation but loses to immediate-witness querying on the primary metric.
Do not promote it to a main-method contribution. See
`literature/structural-followup.md` and `algorithm-notes.md` for evidence and scope.
