# Sources

E007 independently implements classical score-distribution dynamic programming
and reverse marginal messages; it does not claim these inference ideas as new.
E008/E009 derive comparison identities from EC2 and implement original research
prototypes. Additional consulted primary sources are listed in
`literature/structural-followup.md`; no source paper prose or implementation was
copied into these modules.

AmbiQT is obtained from https://github.com/testzer0/AmbiQT, with its MIT license and original Spider provenance retained in the external upstream checkout. This repository's adapter is independently written and does not vendor their evaluation code or database records. It reports a distinct controlled stress endpoint rather than the official benchmark score.

ACL style files reside in the separate paper repository, unmodified from https://github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files. Their source commit and hashes are recorded there. They are not relicensed as original project code.

Publisher-provided BibTeX records and Crossref metadata are retained for citation verification. Research PDFs remain outside Git. Model critique files are explicitly unverified proposals, not endorsed scientific findings.
