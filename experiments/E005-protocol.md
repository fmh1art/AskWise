# E005: partition-aware action certificate (exploratory amendment)

Registered 2026-10-29 after seeing E004. Independent edge bounds save little: 15.39/16 executions for comparison acquisition, worse than random 15.27/16. Do not conceal this failure or call this study independent confirmation.

Replace independent disagreement-edge relaxation by a partition-consistent star bound: an unexecuted world's answer can match at most one known answer class; jointly minimize all its incident known-label edges over matching a known class or taking a new value. Still relax pairs of unexecuted worlds independently. Every true labeling remains feasible, so resulting pairwise-gain lower bounds are sound and at least as tight as independent edge bounds. No unmeasured speedup or novelty is asserted.

Repeat E004 on exactly the same 300 development tasks, three acquisition rules, and all hidden worlds. Verify exact EC2 trace fidelity exhaustively on all three-bit Boolean functions before running. Success requires a material execution-count improvement against the independent-bound random baseline, not merely full upfront execution; also report policy computation overhead. Further confirmation and prior-art review are required before a paper claim.
